## 3.1 S.A. vs S.L. – choosing

**Q: Minimum share capital: **S.A.** vs **S.L.**?**

**S.A.: €60,000** · **S.L.: €3,000**

> Exam answer = the notes' figures. (Real-life update: Ley 18/2022 "Crea y Crece" allows an S.L. with **€1**, with extra safeguards – but answer **€3,000** in this course.)


**Q: How much capital must be **paid up at incorporation**?**

**S.L.: 100%** (fully paid). **S.A.: at least 25%** of each share's par value – the rest is **called-up capital (dividendos pasivos)** paid later (art. 79 LSC).

> Mock Q16(a) "capital always needs to be fully paid-up" → **false** (S.A. allows 25%).


**Q: When **must** founders choose an S.A.?**

- Capital **< €60,000** → must be an **S.L.**
- Shares to be **listed** on a stock exchange → must be an **S.A.**
- Otherwise free choice: S.A. = more formalities but **free transfer**; S.L. = fewer formalities, presumes **stable membership**.


**Q: S.L. vs S.A.: quantitative vs qualitative importance?**

S.L. dominate **quantitatively** (SMEs, low capital, stable members). S.A. dominate **qualitatively** (large corporations, stock market, free transfer of shares).


**Q: S.A. vs S.L. – non-cash contributions, transfer, attendance, votes?**

- Non-cash: **S.A. expert report** required (as a rule); **S.L. no report**
- Shares (S.A.) = **negotiable securities**; stakes (S.L.) **not**
- Transfer: **S.A. free** (by-laws may restrict); **S.L. restricted**
- Attendance: **S.L. cannot be restricted**; S.A. may require a **minimum number of shares**
- Votes: **S.L. one per stake** (unless by-laws); **S.A. per nominal value**


**Q: Board size and directors' term: S.A. vs S.L.?**

Board: **min 3** in both; max **12 in S.L.**, **no max** in S.A.
Term: **S.A. max 6 years** (re-electable for the same max periods); **S.L. may be indefinite**.



## 3.2 Deed of incorporation & by-laws

**Q: How is a capital company incorporated?**

By a **deed of incorporation** executed by the founders **before a notary public**, then **registered in the Mercantile Registry** (Registro Mercantil) of the province of the registered office → **legal personality** as a capital company.

> Mind map steps: **name certificate** (Central Registry) → **tax number (NIF)** → **by-laws** → **notary** → **registry**.


**Q: Who are the **founders**? Minimum number of founders?**

The persons (natural or legal) who **execute the deed** and **subscribe all shares / take up all stakes**. **No minimum** – one person can found an **S.A.U.** or **S.L.U.** (single-member company).

> Wording: you **subscribe shares** (S.A.) but **take up / assume stakes** (S.L.).


**Q: Why do single-member companies exist? What if a company becomes single-member later?**

To overcome the **universal liability** of the sole trader: one person runs a business but is liable only with the company's assets. If it becomes single-member later → **deed declaring sole proprietorship** + registration. Same regime, some **added formalities**.


**Q: Minimum content of the **deed of incorporation** (art. 22 LSC)?**

- **Identity** of founders + will to form an S.A./S.L.
- **Contributions** of each + numbering of shares/stakes
- The **by-laws**
- Initial organisation of the **administration** and identity of directors
- S.A.: approximate **incorporation expenses**


**Q: What are the **by-laws** (estatutos sociales / articles of association)?**

The **rules of organisation and operation** of the company; part of the deed, registered → **binding** on members and directors.


**Q: Minimum content of the **by-laws** (art. 23 LSC)?**

1. **Company name** (+ S.A./S.L.)
2. **Corporate purpose**
3. **Registered office** (in Spain)
4. **Capital** (in euros; S.A.: unpaid part)
5. **Shares/stakes** (number, par value…)
6. **Structure of management body** & GM operation
7. **Ancillary commitments** (if any)


**Q: Rules for the **company name**?**

Must include **S.A.** or **S.R.L./S.L.**; must not be **identical** to an existing company's name nor contrary to public order → certificate from the **Central Mercantile Registry**.


**Q: Where must the **registered office** be?**

In **Spanish territory**, where the **effective administration and management** is, or where the main establishment/centre of operations is.


**Q: Which defaults apply if the by-laws say nothing?**

**Duration: unlimited**; **start of operations: date of the deed**; **fiscal year ends 31 December**.



## 3.3 Company in formation & registration

**Q: What is a **company in formation**? Which acts bind the company after registration (art. 36 LSC)?**

The period between the deed and registration. Acts **necessary for registration** bind the company automatically; **other acts** bind it only if it **expressly accepts** them after registration – otherwise those who acted are liable.


**Q: Deadline to register? Consequence of missing it?**

**One year**. Otherwise it becomes an **irregular company**: any member may request **dissolution**, liquidation and refund of contributions (arts. 39–40 LSC). If it already started business: **general-partnership liability** of members for debts incurred before registration.


**Q: Why is publicity through the Registry so important for capital companies?**

Because members are **not personally liable**, third parties can only rely on the **company's assets**. They need reliable info on **capital, directors, accounts** → transparency and **legal security**. Registry = **filter of lawfulness + publicity**.


**Q: Which later acts must also be registered?**

**Amendments of by-laws**, **appointment/removal of directors**, **annual accounts** (deposit) and many other corporate acts.



## 3.4 Contributions

**Q: What can be contributed to capital? What can **never** be?**

**Cash** or any **asset/right with economic value** (real estate, machinery, trademarks, credit rights, goodwill…). **Never work or services** (art. **58.2 LSC**).

> Mock Q10(b) "work by a member can be a non-cash contribution in an S.L." → **false**. Correct: (d) cash or non-cash in both S.A. and S.L.


**Q: How are **cash contributions** proven?**

The notary attaches a **bank certificate** proving the deposit in an account in the name of the company in formation. Contributions in **euros**.


**Q: Valuation safeguards for **non-cash contributions**?**

**S.A. & S.L.**: contributing member is **liable** to company, members and third parties for the **reality and value** (art. 30 LSC).
**S.A. additionally**: **independent expert report** appointed by the **Registrar** (art. 67 LSC), annexed to the deed (exceptions art. 69).


**Q: What are **ancillary commitments** (prestaciones accesorias)?**

**Services** by members **beyond their capital contribution**, free or paid; **never part of capital** (art. 86 LSC). By-laws must set content, paid/free and consequences of breach.

> This is how Pedro's **work** can enter the company in Case 1 – not as capital, but as an ancillary commitment.



## 3.5 Capital, equity, reserves

**Q: What is **share capital** (capital social)?**

The **total amount of members' contributions**, stated in the by-laws ("nominal capital"). Stable figure.


**Q: What is **equity** (patrimonio neto / real capital)?**

**Assets − liabilities (debts to third parties)** = Capital + reserves (undistributed profits) ± other items (grants, share premium, accumulated losses).

> At incorporation **capital = equity**. Later they diverge: profits kept → equity > capital; losses → equity < capital. Mock Q11 → (d).


**Q: Four principles protecting capital?**

1. **Determination** – always stated in the by-laws; changes only by amending by-laws
2. **Full subscription & minimum payment** – all shares subscribed (S.A. min 25% paid)
3. **Integrity** – par value must match an effective contribution
4. **Minimum outlay** – not below €60,000 / €3,000, at incorporation **and during life**


**Q: Types of **reserves**?**

**Legal** (imposed by law), **statutory** (by-laws), **voluntary** (decided by GM), other special reserves. All come from **undistributed profits** and increase equity.



## 3.6 Members' rights & obligations

**Q: Members' only basic **obligations** after contributing?**

1. Pay **called-up capital** (S.A., if any)
2. Perform **ancillary commitments** (if in by-laws)
Everything else is a **right**, not an obligation.


**Q: Six basic **rights** of members?**

1. **Dividends** (273 LSC)
2. **Liquidation dividend** (391)
3. **Pre-emptive (preferential subscription) right** (304)
4. **Attend and vote** at GM (179)
5. **Challenge** resolutions (204)
6. **Information** (197)

> Mnemonic **D-L-P-V-C-I**: "**D**on't **L**et **P**eople **V**ote **C**areless **I**deas".


**Q: How are **votes** allocated in S.A. vs S.L.?**

**S.A.**: strictly **proportional to par value** of shares (non-voting shares possible).
**S.L.**: **one vote per stake**, but the by-laws may set a different system (e.g. extra votes for certain stakes).



## 3.7 Par, book and fair value

**Q: Define **par (nominal) value**.**

The value the founders **freely assign** to each share/stake in the deed (or in a capital increase deed).


**Q: Define **book value** (valor teórico). Formula?**

The aliquot part of the **company's equity** per share: **Book value = Equity ÷ number of shares**.


**Q: Define **fair (market) value**. Formula?**

The value at which **two independent, informed and interested parties** would exchange the shares: **Fair value = Book value ± expectations** (business valuation).

> Fair value is partly **subjective** (market expectations). Par value = objective fact; book value = accounting calculation.


**Q: Annex example: 75 members, €75,000 capital, 750 shares of €100. Retained earnings €100,000; expectations +€200,000. Book and fair value per share?**

Equity = 75,000 + 100,000 = **€175,000** → BV = 175,000 ÷ 750 = **€233.33**/share (€2,333.33 per member's 10 shares).
FV = 175,000 + 200,000 = **€375,000** → **€500**/share (€5,000 per member).

> Rules: no losses & retained earnings → **BV > PV**; positive expectations → **FV > BV**; negative → FV < BV.
