## Units

1. **The Law, the Rule & the Legal System** – generality & coercibility, BOE + 20 days, sources (law → custom → principles), hierarchy, State vs regions, EU Regulations vs Directives
2. **Business Law & the Entrepreneur** – enterprise vs entrepreneur, legal status, capacity, liability, partnerships vs capital companies, irregular companies, veil lifting
3. **Capital Companies: Incorporation** – S.A. vs S.L., deed & by-laws, registration, contributions, capital vs equity, par/book/fair value
4. **Corporate Bodies I** – general meeting (art. 160, convening, quorum, majorities, universal meeting), four director models
5. **Corporate Bodies II** – duty of care & loyalty, business judgment rule, liability for debts and damages, corporate & individual actions
6. **By-laws: Capital Increase & Reduction** – types of increase, pre-emptive right, share premium, mandatory reduction (2/3), creditor opposition, accordion
7. **Structural Changes & Termination** – mergers (phases, exchange ratio, special mergers), total/partial demerger, segregation, dissolution → liquidation → extinction

Units 8–9 (IP & competition) are **not** part of this midterm and were removed from the app, decks and notes.
